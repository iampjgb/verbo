# Verbo

A Bible-study web app: read, compare versions side by side, study with Hebrew and Greek, commentaries, maps and guided study, and keep notes. It installs on phones, tablets and computers and works offline.

## Use it
Open the site address on any device:
- **Android, or Chrome/Edge on a computer:** choose **Install** (in Settings, or in the browser's address bar).
- **iPhone or iPad:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Bible texts save to the device automatically. Extra study sets (Hebrew and Greek, commentaries, maps) can be saved in **Settings → Offline library**.

## What's here
- `index.html`: the whole app
- `bible/`: Bible texts and study data
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icons/`, `screens/`: what makes it installable and offline
- `offline-packs.json`: the study sets offered for offline use

AI features (Ask AI, written studies, cloud sync) work when Verbo runs inside Claude.
