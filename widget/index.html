Verse of the day on your Home Screen

The same verse as Verbo's Home each day. Tap it to open Verbo at that verse.

  1. Install the free app Scriptable from the App Store. Open in App Store
  2. Tap Copy the script below.
  3. Open Scriptable, tap +, paste, and name it Verbo verse (tap the title at the top).
  4. Go to your Home Screen, press and hold an empty spot, tap Edit › Add Widget, choose Scriptable, pick a size and tap Add Widget.
  5. Press and hold the new widget › Edit Widget › Script › Verbo verse. For the World English Bible instead of the KJV, type WEB in Parameter.

Apple only allows App Store apps to add their own widgets, so this uses Scriptable to show Verbo's verse. The verse text loads from Verbo once and is kept on your phone. View the script · Back to Verbo
