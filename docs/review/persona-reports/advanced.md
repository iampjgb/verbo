# Verbo review: advanced Bible-study user (pastor / seminary student)

Tested live in Chrome (Playwright) at 1366x900, 1920x1080, 200% zoom (683x450 CSS px) and phone 390x844. Server on port 8103, now stopped. Nothing in /home/claude/verbo was changed. Screenshots and scripts are in (local test folder, not in repo)  and (local test folder, not in repo) scripts/.

## 1. Summary

- Study depth is strong for a free PWA: Hebrew and Greek interlinear (RTL, correct lang tags), a Strong's lexicon popup with usage lists, MH, Calvin, Easton, Nave, a 9-version parallel view, red letters, footnotes, and very fast search (0.2 to 0.6 s).
- Biggest correctness bugs: Strong's search fails for every number below 1000 (G25, G26, H430, H1), and parallel view misaligns or hides verses for 65 DRC Psalms and for the Romans 14/16 doxology in WEB.
- Biggest structural gaps: the URL never records chapter or verse (the hash is rewritten to `#reader`), and modal dialogs do not take keyboard focus.
- Keyboard users get Ctrl+K, left/right arrows and j/k, but most are undiscoverable, and selecting a verse by keyboard drops focus to the page body.
- Offline is mostly honest ("Finish saving" states, clear commentary message), but the Words tab spins forever offline and only KJV is auto-saved.

## 3. What works well

- Interlinear view: Hebrew in `lang="he"` with right-to-left rows, Greek in `lang="grc"`, per-word gloss and morphology, pronunciation and accent toggles, and the verse number on the correct side.
- Lexicon popup: lemma, transliteration, Strong's number, form, lexicon text with licence line, and a usage list ("Used 143 times in 110 verses"). Double-click any English word opens its Greek or Hebrew entry.
- Search: global Ctrl+K with grouped results, phrase search in quotes, AND, Strong's numbers (for 1000 and above), Greek and Hebrew input, per-version counts, 9 versions, all in under 0.7 s.
- Parallel view: row-aligned two columns on desktop and phone, swap and close buttons, a compare option for Hebrew and Greek, and "Not in this translation's base text" placeholders (wording needs work, see A7).
- Commentary: concise Matthew Henry shown at the verse, full MH and Calvin on demand with size warnings ("about 2 MB"), Calvin split by verse with Scripture links, Easton and Nave in the panel.
- Share copies the verse with quotes, reference, version and a link. Deep links with `?ref=` work. Reader options cover red letters (also work over BSB), footnotes, focus mode, fullscreen, text size and line spacing.
- Offline library: clear sizes, progress and "Finish saving" for partial sets, storage use shown, and a plain explanation of why only the current Bible is saved. Translation licences (Biblica CC BY-SA, Door43, public domain) are stated in "About these translations".
- Performance for long chapters and chapter switching is very good (see A19). No horizontal scroll at 200% zoom or on phone.
