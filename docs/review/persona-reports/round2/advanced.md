# Verbo round 2: advanced user review (pastor / seminary student)

Branch `review-fixes`, tested live on Chrome desktop 1366x900 and 1920x1080, phone 390x844, plus data checks against `bible/*`. Screenshots and scripts are in `(local test folder, not in repo) `.

## 1. Summary

- The chapter deep dive is a real step up: Henry outline first, setting, parallels, cross references, words and commentary on one page, axe clean, no page errors on 13 chapters.
- It is slow and unstable while loading: 40 to 90 re-renders per open, 0.8 to 2.9 s of blocked main thread, and content above your reading position grows so the page jumps.
- Several "key" picks are weak: nine of 13 chapters show verse 1 as the only key verse, key words are the first 16 lemmas, people are sorted by fame, and the top-8 cross references miss the best NT quotations.
- Attribution gaps for Theographic (CC BY-SA), Calvin, Spurgeon and the red-letter source. No deep link, so reload and Back lose the overview.
- Scores (1 to 10): ease of use 7, UI/UX 7, accessibility 6, intuitiveness 6, study depth 5, Chapter overview 6.

## 3. Design notes

### Item 6: parallel verse alignment

1. Problem: the compare view pairs verses by number, so DRC Psalms, WEB Romans 14 and moved verses misalign or are hidden.
2. Add one build-time map, `bible/align/{tr}.json`, per translation: `{ "Psalms 22": {"1":"0","2":"1"}, "Romans 14": {"23":"23","24":"23"} }` from translation verse number to KJV verse (title or superscription = "0").
3. Where: `chFull(tr,book,ch)` returns `{v}`; add `alignedFull(tr,book,ch)` that re-keys `v` to KJV numbers and keeps `orig` (the translation's own number) in a parallel object.
4. Compare renderer (the `cmp` layout in `renderVerses`) loops KJV verse numbers, asks `alignedFull` for each translation, and joins all verses mapped to the same KJV number. It prints the translation's own number as a small superscript ("v. 2 DRC").
5. Verses with no KJV partner (WEB Rom 14:24-26) are shown after the last verse under "Extra in this translation", never hidden. "Not in this translation" is only shown when the map says so.
6. Psalm titles: show as an unnumbered line above verse 1 in every translation.
7. Build the maps from OSIS versification (DRC to KJV and WEB to KJV differences are small: 65 psalms and a few NT chapters), then add a unit test that every verse of every translation is reachable exactly once.
8. Verse select, highlight keys and cross references stay in KJV numbering. Use `orig` only for display.
9. Test: Psalms 22 and 51, Romans 14 and 16, 3 John 14-15, Malachi 3-4; compare counts before and after.

### Item 7: deep links and keeping your place

1. Hash format: `#/read/John.3.16-18` for the reader, `#/chapter/Acts.13` for the overview, `#/search?q=...`. Keep `?ref=` as a fallback.
2. Write on every change with `history.pushState` for chapter and page changes and `replaceState` for verse select and scroll. Today `location.hash` is fixed at `#reader` and `#chapter`.
3. One router: `route(hash)` parses, validates with `validRef`, then calls `openRef` or `A.chOv`. Call it from `popstate` and at boot, so Back and reload work.
4. Keep your place: store `S.reading.pos={ch, v, off}` where `v` is the first verse in view (use an IntersectionObserver on `.v`) and `off` its pixel offset. Save at most once per 500 ms and on `visibilitychange`. Restore after the chapter renders, before the first paint if possible.
5. Ranges: parse `16-18` and `16,18` into `ui.sel` as a set and highlight all of them. Outline rows should pass `ref:start-end` (A2-13).
6. "Continue reading" uses `S.reading.pos`, not just book and chapter.
7. Overview: `#/chapter/Acts.13/connections` scrolls to a section id. The tab strip updates the hash with `replaceState`.
8. Back from the reader after an outline tap returns to the overview at the same scroll.
9. Share button: copy the canonical URL plus the reference text.
10. Tests: reload, Back x3, link into a range, invalid link shows a toast and opens the nearest valid chapter.

## 4. What works well

- The deep dive order (Outline, Key verses, Setting, Connections, Words, Commentary, Notes) matches how a pastor prepares a text. Empty sections and tabs are hidden.
- Henry outline rows jump straight to the verse in the reader with no popup. Clicking 14-41 in Acts 13 opened 13:14.
- Parallels are correct where they exist: Matthew 26 has nine story pairings in right verse ranges, and Zechariah 13:7, Psalms 22:1 and Isaiah 53:7-8 render the right KJV text side by side.
- Journeys by chapter are right for Acts 13 to 21 and 27 to 28 (paul1 to paul4).
- "Words of Jesus" counts equal the keys in `red.json` (Matthew 26: 33, Mark 14: 27, Luke 22: 36, John 13: 23, Revelation 22: 5) and the Gospels and Acts counts look right.
- Cross reference previews with the "from verse N" label and the OpenBible.info credit are clear. My offline recomputation matches the app's top 8.
- Accessibility basics are good: `lang="grc"` and `lang="he"` on original words, visible 3 px focus ring, correct heading order, axe clean on the overview with accordions open, accordion open state survives redraws.
- Spurgeon appears for Psalms, Calvin loads on demand, and Strong's search now works for numbers under 1000.
- Rapid prev and next is stable and ends on the right chapter with no errors.
- Phone layout has no horizontal overflow on Acts 13, Matthew 26 and Psalms 119; all buttons are 36 px or taller except two inline links (Easton and Nave rows are 21 px tall).
