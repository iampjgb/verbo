# Verbo round 2: beginner review (review-fixes branch)

Tester role: first-time Bible app user, older, weak eyesight, phone first (390x844, touch), one desktop pass (1366x900). Chrome via Playwright. Screenshots are in (local test folder, not in repo) . Scripts are in (local test folder, not in repo) scripts/. /home/claude/verbo was not changed.

## 1. Summary

- The Chapter overview is a big step up. It is short, calm, and the order (Outline, Key verses, Setting, Connections, Words, Commentary) makes sense. A beginner can tell what it is for within one screen.
- The main problems are navigation, not looks: tabs hide when the top bar shows, scroll place is lost on Back, header Back skips the overview, and prev/next never crosses a book edge.
- Jargon is the second problem: Easton, Nave, Key words with G-codes, "Cross references", "OpenBible.info", a long credit line.
- F1 to F8 all pass on a phone. F2 focus in the note box is lost after about 1 second (unconfirmed on a real phone).
- Scores (1 to 10): ease of use 7, UI/UX 7, accessibility 6, intuitiveness 6. Chapter overview alone: 7 (clear and tidy, but it loses you on the way back and uses study jargon).

Scope notes. Tested chapters: Genesis 1, Psalms 23, John 3, Matthew 26, Acts 13, plus Revelation 22, Obadiah 1, Jude 1, Psalms 119, Exodus 1, Matthew 1, Matthew 28. No page errors. axe on the overview (Acts 13 and Genesis 1, all accordions open): zero violations. The 403 console errors come from Google hosts blocked by this sandbox's proxy, not from the app.

## 5. What works well

- The overview answers "what is this chapter?" at once: big title, a one-line subtitle (for example "The Creation"), a short fact list, then a full-width "Read Genesis 1" button. "Guided study" is a quiet link. This is easy for an older reader.
- Type is large and readable. Sizes are clear: 32px title, 21px section names, 16px body. Dark mode is clean.
- The Outline is the right first section. Each row is a plain sentence with a verse range, and a tap lands in the reader at that verse with no popup in the way (a3-outline-row.png). Back returns to the overview.
- Empty sections vanish, so Psalms 119 and one-chapter books are not padded. Revelation 22 and Genesis 1 simply omit the missing neighbour.
- Show all N, accordions, chips, parallels, cross reference rows and the Easton and Nave sheets all did what the label said. Peek sheets close with Back.
- Cross references show the verse text and "from verse N", so a beginner can read them without leaving the page.
- Journeys are a highlight. The Paul journey card is clear and friendly (a4-journey.png).
- Offline works after the first load: the whole overview filled in with no network (a6-offline-Matthew26.png).
- "Words of Jesus: 17 verses" is a nice touch for John 3.
- F1 to F8 all work. The "Read Romans 8 as text" button is a clear way out of comic mode.
- axe found no violations on the overview, and the tab row uses `aria-current`.
