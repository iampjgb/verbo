# Verbo UX review: regular daily Bible reader

Tested at 390x844 (main), 820x1180 and 1366x900, Chrome via Playwright, axe-core, offline mode. Screenshots are in (local test folder, not in repo) . Scripts are in (local test folder, not in repo) scripts/. No files in /home/claude/verbo were changed.

## 1. Summary

- Reading, version switching, search, plans, dark mode and offline KJV are strong and fast.
- On phones and small tablets (width under about 900) the verse popup is broken for three of its six buttons. Highlight and Note do nothing visible. Save writes a corrupt bookmark that then crashes the Bookmarks page.
- Data persistence is good (highlights, notes, settings survive reload) except bookmarks, scroll position, and silent loss when storage is full.
- Keyboard and screen reader support is weak: verses expose only "Verse N", dialogs do not take focus or trap it.
- Scores (1-10): Ease of use 6, UI/UX 7, Accessibility 5, Intuitiveness 5.

Tested and fine (no finding): note with `<script>`, `<img onerror>`, emoji and a 5,000 character body is escaped, saved, listed and survives reload. Two tabs writing a note and a highlight kept both. "Today" verse follows local date by timezone. Daily verse "Next day" is disabled on today. Axe colour-contrast found nothing in light or dark on home and reader with highlights.

Not tested or unconfirmed: real Web Share sheet on a phone (headless used clipboard fallback: "Copied. Paste it into a message."). Install prompt behaviour on a real device (Install button present in Settings). Reduced-motion behaviour: CSS has 5 `prefers-reduced-motion` rules but I did not visually verify them. Prev-day limit on the Today card: 39 taps back, no end reached (unconfirmed whether a limit exists).

## 3. What works well

- Fast, clear Today screen: date-based verse, EN/TL toggle, share as image, Study button, plans and "Open the Bible" on first screen.
- Book and chapter picker accepts typing ("acts 8:37", "john 3") and jumps to the verse. Very quick for people who know the book order.
- Version switching keeps your place. A verse missing from a version (Acts 8:37 in BSB) shows "This verse is not in this translation's base text" and the footnote explains why. Clean handling.
- Search is quick (about 1 second), spans verses, topics, dictionary, parallels and notes, finds Tagalog ("pag-ibig" 147 verses), supports reference typing ("Roma 8:28") and quoted phrases. Empty state is clear: "Nothing found ... Search commentaries too".
- Multi-verse select with Copy, Share, labelled colour highlight and Note works well.
- Highlight change colour and Remove work. Highlights, notes and settings persist across reload.
- Notes editor autosaves on Back, escapes HTML, handles 5,000 characters.
- Dark mode is well tuned, highlights stay readable in dark, no contrast failures from axe.
- Offline: service worker installs, the shell and KJV work offline after first load, search works offline, and the failure message for uncached versions is present (see R10 for the wording issue).
- Reading plans: Start plan opens Day 1 at once, completes the day at chapter end, offers "Read day 2" and a quiz.
- AI features are explained plainly: "Ask AI works when Verbo is opened inside the Claude app. Here only the sample questions have answers."
- Onboarding is a skippable bottom sheet and does not return after Skip or reload (`verbo.welcomed`). It does return after storage is cleared, as expected.
