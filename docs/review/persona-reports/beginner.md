# Verbo review: beginner persona

Tested on a 390x844 phone viewport (main pass) and 1366x900 desktop, Chrome, fresh contexts. Scripts are in (local test folder, not in repo) scripts. Screenshots are in (local test folder, not in repo) . Line numbers refer to index.html.

## 1. Summary

A beginner can get to John 3:16 or Psalm 23 in two taps and a few typed letters. Search accepts "jn 3 16", "Ps23", "1 cor 13" and jumps to the verse and highlights it. Text size is easy to find and goes up to 32px.
The weak spots are small. The welcome tour names a "Read" tab that does not exist. Misspelled book names dead-end. The end of the Bible gives no feedback. Several controls are icon-only or smaller than 44px. A screen reader cannot read the verses.
Scores (1-10): Ease of use 8. UI/UX 7.5. Accessibility 5.5. Intuitiveness 7.
AI features (Ask AI, cloud sync) explain themselves well when unavailable ("Sample answer here · full answers in the Claude app", "Needs the app to run inside Claude"). Not counted as broken.

## 3. What works well

- Search is forgiving for good input: "jn 3 16", "John 3:16", "psalm 23", "Psalms 23", "1 cor 13", "Ps23", "Jn3:16" all give a "Go to" list. Enter jumps to the verse and highlights it (17_john316.png).
- The book picker groups books as Gospels, Paul's letters and so on. It has a typed box with "like jn 3", and a chapter grid with big buttons (08_picker.png). It remembers Recent passages.
- The Aa sheet is clear. A+ and A- have full labels. A live preview shows "For God so loved the world". The size goes from 15 to 32px with no horizontal scroll. The choice survives a reload.
- First launch is short: three steps with Skip. The welcome is a labelled dialog. It works in landscape and desktop.
- One-chapter books work: Jude and Obadiah open fine. Next from Jude goes to Revelation 1 and Previous from Obadiah goes to Amos 9.
- Offline: KJV chapters (Romans 8, Psalm 23, Ezekiel 5), search, and the app shell all worked after one online visit. The error state has a "Try again" button.
- Dark mode and reduced motion are supported. Axe contrast is clean in light mode and in most dark screens. The viewport allows pinch zoom.
- AI features say clearly when they need the Claude app.
- Help has "Getting started", "Words explained" and "Show the welcome again".
- The verse menu on the phone is plain and labelled: Highlight, Note, Save, Study, Copy, Share.
